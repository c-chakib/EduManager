## 🎓 Key Learnings

1. **Content > Design** - Compelling copy drives conversions more than pretty visuals
2. **Social Proof** - Specific numbers and testimonials build trust
3. **Risk Reversal** - "Gratuit 30 jours, aucune carte" removes barriers
4. **Visual Hierarchy** - Guide user's eye to important information
5. **Micro-interactions** - Small animations make the page feel alive
6. **Mobile-First** - Most users browse on mobile, design for that first
